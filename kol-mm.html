SAVI · Market Manager Workspace

How would you like to set up this collaboration?

Two flows live in this workspace, depending on who's initiating the partnership. Pick the one that matches your situation today — you can always come back here from the sidebar.
🚀
Flow A · KOL-initiated
Match an existing KOL travel plan to a hotel
A KOL has submitted a travel plan via the public form, and you'd like to find a hotel partner in your market to host them. You'll work through a 3-step wizard: pick the KOL plan(s), select a hotel + capture the eligibility deal, then review & hand off to admin.
  • Browse incoming KOL applications by region
  • Multi-select to batch-match a group together
  • Capture HID, BL%, sponsored package & eligibility
🏨
Flow B · Hotel-initiated
Submit a hotel sponsor request for admin to map a KOL
A hotel partner has reached out wanting to sponsor a KOL stay, but you don't have a specific creator in mind. Capture the offer once — both the Agoda eligibility (Discount/INM) and the KOL-facing sponsor details — and admin will shortlist suitable creators from the application pool.
  • One single-page form, MM submits on behalf of the hotel
  • Splits the offer into Agoda eligibility + KOL sponsor details
  • Optional voucher giveaway block for KOL audience promos
💡
Both flows ultimately land in the Admin Portal → Match Plans screen so the same Deliverables / Price Pre-check pipeline picks them up — the only difference is who provided the inputs first.

Hotel Matching
💰 Heads up — every match is subject to a post-stay Price Pre-check (pvar)
On day 8 after KOL checkout, admin runs a price-uplift check on this hotel (by HID, 7-day window post-checkout) against the pre-stay baseline. The result decides whether the KOL's deliverables can publish on the planned date:
  • ✅ Pass — deliverables publish as planned. No further action needed from you.
  • 🔴 Fail — you'll receive a "pre-check failed" email from admin. You have 2 business days to negotiate a pricing adjustment with the hotel. A re-check will run ~10 days later (2 BD adjustment + 7-day window + day-8 buffer). The KOL is not notified of any delay unless the hotel can't adjust in time.

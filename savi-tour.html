Step by step

Detailed walkthroughs

Click each role to expand the full step-by-step flow.

🎬
KOL / Creator — applying for sponsorship
Uses: kol-ext.html (public, shareable)
3 steps ▾
1
Creator profile
Fill in name, nationality, contact email, and select social platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, XHS, etc.). Enter follower count and profile URL per platform. Confirm you've read the program requirements — this is a required checkbox before proceeding.
AI validates email format & URL
2
Travel details
Select target region and city, enter travel dates and number of guests. Optionally add flight info (airline, flight number, arrival time). An eligibility reminder shows the partnership criteria for the target market — the KOL sees this before submitting.
AI checks date format & region
3
Content plan & submit
Describe the planned content — e.g. "1 × Reel + 3 × Stories + 1 × Feed post". Add any notes (dietary requirements, etc.). Hit Submit — a reference number is generated and the submission is saved to Google Sheets automatically.
Auto-syncs to Sheets → MM sees it instantly
🚀
Market Manager — Flow A: Match a KOL to a hotel
Uses: kol-mm.html → KOL Applications (sidebar)
3 steps ▾
1
Browse & select KOL applications
Open MM Workspace and click "KOL Applications" in the sidebar. See all incoming applications synced from Google Sheets, filterable by region. Select one or multiple KOL plans to match together, then click "Start Hotel Matching".
KOL data pre-filled — no re-typing
2
Fill hotel & eligibility details
Enter the matched hotel name, HID, Agoda URL, and BL%. Set the Agoda eligibility commitment (Discount % or INM %). Then fill in the KOL-facing sponsor package — sponsored room type, extras (breakfast, social bar), and content deliverables.
AI validates HID format & URL
3
Review & submit to Admin
Review the full match summary — KOL profile, hotel details, eligibility, and sponsor package. Submit and it immediately appears in the Admin Portal under "Match Plans" for confirmation.
Syncs to Admin → Match Plans instantly
🏨
Market Manager — Flow B: Hotel wants to sponsor a KOL
Uses: kol-mm.html → Hotel Sponsor Form (sidebar)
1 form ▾
1
Submit a Hotel Sponsor Request
A hotel partner wants to sponsor a KOL stay, but no specific creator is assigned yet. MM fills one single-page form: hotel info (HID, BL%), Agoda eligibility commitment, KOL-facing sponsor package (room, extras), and optionally a voucher giveaway block for KOL audience promotions.
Appears in Admin → Hotel Requests for KOL mapping
⚙️
Admin / Supply Team — managing the full pipeline
Uses: kol-admin.html · Password protected 🔒
6 sections ▾
1
Hotel Requests — review hotel-initiated sponsorships
See all sponsor requests submitted by MMs on behalf of hotels. Each request shows HID, BL%, proposed KOL sponsor package, and eligibility commitment. Map a suitable KOL from the application pool to complete the match.
From: MM Flow B
2
KOL Applications — browse the incoming pool
Read-only view of all KOL applications synced from Google Sheets. Filter by region, platform, or status. Use this to manually identify creators for hotel requests.
From: KOL kol-ext.html submissions
3
Match Plans — confirm and notify
The main action screen. See all matched plans from MMs. For each match: confirm deliverables (content format + deadline), then click "Notify KOL" — a pre-drafted personalised email is copied to clipboard, addressed to the KOL's email. Once deliverables are confirmed, click "Notify MM" to copy the full handoff email addressed to the MM.
Emails pre-drafted & addressed automatically
4
Deliverables Tracking — monitor content submissions
Track all confirmed collaborations and their content delivery status. Update status as content is submitted by creators. Useful for QBR reporting and follow-up.
5
Ambassador Applications — separate program intake
Applications from the kol-ambassador.html form land here. These are creators applying for the commission-based Ambassador program (distinct from the Creator / sponsorship program).
From: kol-ambassador.html
6
Performance Tracking — BL% analytics & SQL campaign table
Two tools in one screen:

BL% by Country & Quarter — select any combination of markets (TW / HK / CN / SG / MY) and a quarter range to see the total BL% per market per quarter. Data is auto-populated from confirmed Match Plans.

Campaign Table — a structured table of all confirmed campaigns with Region (htl), HID, MM, Hotel Name, Start Date (first content publish date), and End Date (start + 13 days). Use this to run SQL / Metabase pvar uplift queries. One-click Copy as CSV button included.
Auto-populated from Match Plans → confirmed deliverables

FAQ

Common questions

Do KOLs need to create an account?
No. kol-ext.html is fully public — just share the link. No login, no account setup.
If a KOL submits on their phone, can the MM see it on their computer?
Yes. Data is synced to Google Sheets in real time, so any device can see the latest submissions as long as there's an internet connection.
What's the difference between Flow A and Flow B in MM Workspace?
Flow A = a KOL submitted first, and the MM finds a hotel to match them. Flow B = a hotel reached out first wanting to sponsor a creator, and Admin will find a suitable KOL from the pool.
What does "Notify KOL" do in the Admin portal?
It generates a personalised email (with the KOL's name, hotel, dates, and proposed package) and copies it to your clipboard — already addressed to the KOL's email. You just paste and send.
What's the Ambassador form for?
kol-ambassador.html is a separate intake for creators applying to the commission-based Ambassador Program — different from the sponsored-stay Creator Program. Both land in the Admin portal under their respective sections.
Can I update the form fields or add new categories?
Yes — the HTML files are in GitHub. Make changes there and Vercel will redeploy automatically within 1–2 minutes.

What is the Agoda Creator Program?

The Agoda Creator Program is an exclusive, content-for-stay initiative designed for influential travel creators. Unlike the standard Ambassador program (which focuses on commissions), the Creator Program aims to support your high-quality content production by providing sponsored hotel accommodations.

Our goal is to help you reduce travel costs while you create authentic, engaging stories for your audience.

How It Works:

  1. Submit Your Plan: Share your upcoming travel itinerary with us at least 1.5 months in advance.
  2. We Match for You: Agoda will reach out to our extensive network of partner hotels in your destination to secure a sponsorship.
  3. Stay & Create: Enjoy a sponsored stay (minimum 1 night guaranteed upon successful match) and share your experience through high-quality digital content.

Please review these guidelines before proceeding:

  • Eligible Regions: Currently focusing on travel within Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau, China, Singapore, and Malaysia.
  • Sponsorship Details: For any trip, we guarantee at least one night of complimentary stay in exchange for at least one 30-second short-form video (Reels/Shorts/TikTok). Additional nights or perks (e.g., breakfast, social bar access) depend on the hotel partner's discretion and will be discussed before confirmation.
  • Guest Policy: Sponsorship is generally limited to 2 guests. Family-oriented creators travelling with children may be discussed on a case-by-case basis.
  • Verification: For international trips, proof of flight booking is required once the sponsorship is confirmed to prevent "no-show" situations.
  • Availability: Please note that sponsorship is not 100% guaranteed as it depends on hotel room availability, seasonal demand, and marketing quotas.
